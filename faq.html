Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about renting with BM Connect

Simply browse our listings, select a property you're interested in, and click "Inquire Now" to fill out an inquiry form. Our team will contact you within 24 hours to schedule a viewing and guide you through the application process.

We require a deposit (usually one month's rent) along with the first month's rent before move-in. Payments can be made via bank transfer or secure online payment. All transactions are documented with official receipts.

If you need to cancel your application before signing the lease, your deposit is fully refundable within 14 days. After lease signing, standard notice periods apply as per your rental agreement.

Tenants can request maintenance through our online portal, by email at maintenance@bmconnect.co.za, or by calling our office. We aim to respond to all maintenance requests within 48 hours.
